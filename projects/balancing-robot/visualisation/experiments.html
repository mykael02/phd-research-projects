Discrete experiments, not an interpolated boundary

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom and hover over a point for its recorded metrics. Use the toolbar to reveal hidden points. Marker shape and colour both encode outcome. Torque is plotted on its actual numeric scale, including the unequal intervals. The image-download control saves the current view.

Each point is one deterministic trial. The 3D view does not estimate a continuous stability surface or a probability of recovery. Occlusion can hide points; use the complete 2D maps for precise comparisons. A planned push may never have been delivered if the robot fell before 2 s.

Recovered means a final dwell of at least 1 s within ±1° tilt, ±0.03 m displacement and ±0.03 m/s speed. Unsettled means upright without satisfying that recovery criterion. Fallen means the 45° limit was reached at a sampled endpoint.
